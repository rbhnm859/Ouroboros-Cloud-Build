V68 is an evidence-preserving reconstruction, not another global filter stack.

- Preserve the V52/V67-A execution path as the supply spine.
- Family contracts are overlay/veto/routing refinements; they may not silently replace a positive parent cohort.
- Introduce a CanonicalFamilyId emitted at detection and carried unchanged through candidate, route, grid, basket, close, and report telemetry.
- Fail closed if a closed basket lacks one of exactly 12 canonical family IDs.
- Separate identity family from execution route; parser must never infer family by splitting human-readable labels.
- Family-native Grid is challenger-only until each family×route proves positive marginal net with non-degrading PF/expectancy and legal risk.
- AB=CD standalone capital remains off unless its own preregistered evidence gate passes; AB=CD completion/confluence remains available to parent families.
- Preserve Rat/Shark/Cypher positive parent lanes as protected reference cohorts during reconstruction; protection means causal comparison, not guaranteed admission.
- Scheduler ranks only already-admissible theses; it cannot create Alpha or erase canonical identity.
- No cTrader CLI source mutation. Alpha source is staged, statically validated, hashed, then atomically replaced.
