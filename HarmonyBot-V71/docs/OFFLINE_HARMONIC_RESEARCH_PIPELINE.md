# Offline Harmonic Research Pipeline

This pipeline is research-only and uses only burned 2021–2023 calibration evidence.

- Truth layer: `build_harmonic_event_atlas.py` creates a deterministic event atlas from governed SHADOW_PREPASS outputs.
- Diagnostic layer: `analyze_family_resolution_atlas.py` measures family/year structural outcomes and concentration without fitting a selector.
- `native_outcome_r/native_result` are explicitly excluded as research targets because V71 records them as V51 native proxies, not independent counterfactual payoffs.
- The next permitted alpha experiment is an actual family-native causal state machine, Grid OFF, 1% risk, burned 2021–2023 only.
- Validation and Fresh remain locked.

The atlas never changes capital eligibility, core scheduling, risk, execution, detector ratios, or the frozen harmonic hash.

## Family-native causal experiment

The first post-Atlas alpha hypothesis is `R_V72_FAMILY_NATIVE_CAUSAL`:

- family-native completed-bar proof closes become the reversal research entry anchor;
- the original structural invalidation remains the stop;
- family canonical T1/T2 supplies the reversal target and must still satisfy cost-adjusted Net RR >= 2;
- a failed harmonic is not immediately converted into a 0.618 order: it must first close beyond structural invalidation, then on a later completed M1 bar retest that boundary and close in the continuation direction;
- AB=CD standalone and transition/conflict contexts remain shadow-only;
- Grid is off, risk qualification remains 1%, V51 Core remains first, and this experiment has no capital execution path;
- PASS requires every burned year to have at least 39 independent census outcomes, mean R > 0, PF_R > 1, conservative LCB > 0, plus lane non-regression for lanes with at least 20 observations.

A shadow PASS is not V72 promotion. It only authorizes the later execution-conversion/DEV experiment.
